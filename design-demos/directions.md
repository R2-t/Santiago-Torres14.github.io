## The six candidates (three logics × two)

### A · Emission-Line Rail — THE ROLL (assigned, grounded #6)
- **Logic & source:** impeccable catalog world `operate-c-emission-line-rail`, seed #6; board
  opened at `.impeccable/reference/operate-c-emission-line-rail.webp`.
- **Borrowed rules:** procedural charcoal ground banded in graphite; colour exists only as
  1px hairlines at seven named wavelengths; one off-centre calibrated rail carries the whole
  page — every destination is a tick, the open one blooms into a big plate above it; type is
  one condensed grotesque at ranked sizes distinguished by tracking and ink, not weight;
  state = full-height solid (live), dashed (pending), half-height (stale), struck (disabled);
  sodium-yellow doubled line outranks everything.
- **Hero material:** the career itself as an emission spectrum — five roles as five spectral
  lines positioned on a year rail; technologies as wavelength ticks.
- **Skeleton:** horizontal rail across the viewport with role ticks; the selected tick opens
  a wide plate beneath holding that role's real bullets; contact sits as the brightest,
  doubled line.
- **Where the nerve goes:** a recruiter scanning a CV sees an instrument plate, and the only
  saturated thing on the page is the email line.
- **Form from content:** he extracts actionable signal from noisy well data; the layout is
  that operation, drawn once.

### B · Well-Log Section — IMPECCABLE'S PICK (grounded #1)
- **Logic & source:** best-designer logic, Experimental Jetset's systematic technical
  modernism (documentation that behaves like a lab record), applied to the log-print format
  of petrophysical sections.
- **Borrowed rules:** one continuous depth axis as the page spine; sections appear as
  narrow trace bands beside it, never as cards; annotation red reserved for human marks;
  captions at label minimum 12px, dense but legible; paper stock, not screen grey.
- **Hero material:** the real profile photo (`public/images/IMG_PROFILE.JPG`) printed as the
  header plate, and the five roles as logged intervals with measured descriptions.
- **Skeleton:** vertical depth rail (years, 2022→2025 going down) with three trace columns:
  role, technology, evidence; summary paragraph set as the log's header block.
- **Where the nerve goes:** the whole page hangs off one measurement axis; there is no hero.
- **Form from content:** oil-field time-series work is read off a depth track; his career is
  presented the same way.
- **Honest risk:** reads cold and dense; small labels must survive JPEG compression and
  recruiters printing it.

### C · Changelog Column (grounded #2, verified reference)
- **Logic & source:** reality-reference logic. Same-medium comparison: Maggie Appleton's
  personal site (https://maggieappleton.com/, Astro digital garden, verified live this
  session by fetch/search; **not screenshotted** — no Python Playwright on this machine, so
  the borrow is written from its served markup and structure, not from its pixels).
- **Borrowed rules:** work shown as a growing record rather than a portfolio of finished
  objects; explicit epistemic status per entry (published / in progress / planned); prose
  leads, decoration recedes; one column at reading width with a slim meta gutter.
- **Hero material:** the entries themselves — five roles as released versions, projects and
  blog as an empty-but-real stream labelled honestly.
- **Skeleton:** masthead line (name, position, mailto) → release log of roles with dates as
  version stamps → "Writing" section showing zero posts with what will be published first →
  "Projects" section stating nothing is public yet.
- **Where the nerve goes:** shipping absence openly instead of filling it with cards.
- **Form from content:** engineers already keep changelogs; this is his, kept in public.

### D · Glazier Colour-Field Partition (dealt challenger, fused)
- **Logic & source:** impeccable catalog world `craft-making-glazier-colorfield-partition`.
  Board at `.impeccable/reference/craft-making-glazier-colorfield-partition.webp`.
- **Borrowed rules (kept after fusing):** matte black glazing bars as the only line ink;
  clear/seeded-white panes as calm ground; cobalt, amber, oxblood glass only for what matters
  now; one pane one statement, type never crossing a bar; hover backlights a pane.
- **Fused verdict input:** product supplies the panes' contents — email pane, location pane,
  five role panes, skills pane.
- **Why it is weighed seriously:** a recruiter identifies instantly with a wall of framed
  documents; the grid is legible at a glance.
- **Weakness under weighing:** with five roles plus contact plus two future streams, the
  elevation needs ~9 panes; the most important pane (mailto) competes with eight equals, and
  the format flattens the ordering information (dates, durations) a screener actually needs.

### E · Information Mandala (roulette, east #8 Sugiura)
- **Logic & source:** second-hand roulette draw at minute 31 → east slot 8,
  `sugiura-info-mandala`; specimen referenced from the skill library.
- **Borrowed rules:** radial symmetric diagram as the primary carrier; labels packed on
  concentric rings; black ink on paper, one spot colour for the active ring.
- **Hero material:** one mandala mapping his stack — centre "real-time data", rings for
  ingest / transform / serve, spokes naming the actual systems (Cognite CDF, SNS/SQS,
  RabbitMQ, DynamoDB, PostgreSQL, OpenTelemetry).
- **Why it stays a backup:** gorgeous, but a recruiter must decode the diagram before
  learning who he is; strong on identification-by-peers, weak on clarity-for-screeners.

### F · Kinetic Systems Grid (best-designer logic, bold)
- **Logic & source:** Studio Dumbar-style identity systems — motion as the brand grammar,
  split-type headlines, data-like overlays on a hard grid.
- **Borrowed rules:** display type cut and animated along the baseline; monochrome field with
  one industrial accent; sections revealed by translate-on-scroll; numbers and timestamps as
  living decoration.
- **Hero material:** the pipeline itself animated — packets travelling a route from wells to
  dashboards, ending on the mailto.
- **Why it stays a backup:** the strongest dopamine, the highest performance/accessibility
  cost, and motion-heavy pages read as marketing rather than engineering to a cautious
  screener. Also flagged by the appearance ledger as close to how many AI runs ship "dev
  portfolio".
